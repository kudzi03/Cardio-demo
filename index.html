Common questions

What HR and safety officers ask us

How does HRDC reimbursement work?
Our courses are BQA and HRDC accredited, which makes the training expenses reimbursable through HRDC. Your finance team submits the claim; we provide the accredited documentation you need. Ask us on WhatsApp and we'll walk you through it.
Can you train at our site or camp?
Yes — we train locally in Maun, at your premises, or in camp anywhere in Botswana. For in-camp training, accommodation, meals and transport to and from camp are arranged by the client; we bring everything else.
How long are certificates valid?
Certificates are valid for 2 years. We recommend booking refresher training before expiry so your team never has a compliance gap.
Can you handle large groups?
Yes. We scale qualified trainers to your group size, all trained to the same accredited standard — from a small office team to a full site workforce.
How fast can we get a quotation?
Send your course list and headcount by WhatsApp or email and you'll receive a written quotation the same working day.
